Inside the app

What CoinQuest does

  • One number for today

    Your daily budget is what's left this month divided by the days until payday. Spend less today and the days after get a little more. Spend too fast and CoinQuest warns you before your saving goal slips.

  • Months that start on payday

    Each month runs from one payday to the next. See what you saved, what you spent, and which categories it went to.

  • Loans with friends

    Note who you lent to and who you borrowed from. Send a friendly reminder, and settle up in one tap.

  • Tasks and reminders

    Keep simple lists and get reminded on time, with an alarm for the ones you can't miss.

  • Widgets on your home screen

    Log a spend in two taps without opening the app, and tick off tasks right from the widget.

  • Your data stays with you

    No account and no cloud. Everything stays on your phone, behind your fingerprint if you want, and you can back it up to a file.

Install in four steps

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download. If your browser warns about this type of file, tap Download anyway.

  2. Open the file from the download notification or your Downloads folder.

  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps. The switch is called Allow from this source.

  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect says the app is unknown, tap More details, then Install anyway.

When a new version is out, CoinQuest shows an Update button that downloads it. Your data stays.
